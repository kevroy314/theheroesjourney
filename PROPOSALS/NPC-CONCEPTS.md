We have Spite, the best friend, spelled out elsewhere. But given we want our world to feel alive, we need many more NPCs, human and otherwise. Here are a few concepts for NPCs:

1. The mountain woman - she lives in a lone hut and gives highly dangerous quests to retireve items near major bad guys. She sells a few very helpful items including her most expensive item, a map of a labyrinth nearby. She has a best friend in the city who is looking for her. There is an escort quest to bring the city woman to her, but it's punishing. It lowers the price of the map to a reasonable one if you succeed.

2. The happy unhoused man - he wanders around town picking up anything left on the ground, collecting these random items (including player items). He also wanders the nearby countryside along with his 5 twin brothers. Some mobs will force players to drop items, and he will pick these up and sell them back to the player at a price. But he also collects things from elsewhere. His prices are always VERY reasonable (not sarcasm, they actually are). He likes unique items, so he'll pay you more the further from the home town the item is from. He's clearly unhoused by choice because he has a ton of money. Why he doesn't want to live in the empty house in town that clearly belogns to him and his brothers is a hidden tragedy. The 6 brothers married a set of 6 twins (mix of men and women) who all disappeared when the time loop started. They collect items looking for clues to their loves whereabouts. They don't go home because it isn't home without their loved ones.

3. The busybody - an elderly woman who is constantly looking into people's windows. She'll give you quests to go talk to people in town and give you candy if you do (which can be eaten for a temporary speed buff). She's forgetful, so she will rarely give you a candy with some of her medication in it that slows you down to a crawl for 30min. She loves animals, so if you lead an animal to her, she'll give you a candy for free (once per run). She's looking for her cat who is actively avoiding her because she just picks the worst cat food. You can find the cat around town, but if you have candy with you, it will run away because it knows the smell and doesn't want to go home. If you find a fish, you can lead the cat back to the woman, but it cannot have line of sight (you must use buildings to make it a surprise). The cat will scratch you before running away but the woman will give you her secret name, which, if you call her by itt, lets you get a free candy from her any time you see her (candy buffs do not stack). You remember the name in your mind palace as part of her character card.
